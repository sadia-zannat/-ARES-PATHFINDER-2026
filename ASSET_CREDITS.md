The NASA Z2 mesh/texture is the base for six suit accent variants. The 18-bone skin weights and 17 animation clips were authored in the included Blender preparation script. The crew are shared-suit variants, not six unique facial likenesses. ATLAS-6 uses NASA Perseverance geometry, SKYSCOUT uses Ingenuity geometry and ALPHA uses Robonaut2 geometry with original articulated control pivots and an additional procedural two-link tool arm. They are gameplay adaptations, not engineering-accurate replicas of future deployed systems.

Poly Haven aerial_rocks_02 and rock_face_03 textures: CC0; original metadata and license page retained. Mars display colorization is a derivative and not calibrated optical color. Other hardware, terrain reconstructions, sky/dust shaders, UI, flag cloth and audio synthesis are project-authored.

Historical Sol 1 SuperCam audio: NASA/JPL-Caltech/LANL/CNES/CNRS/ISAE-Supaero. https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/scam-mic-sol001-run001.wav

MEDA Sol 36: NASA PDS Atmospheres / Mars 2020 MEDA instrument team. Full CSV and XML label preserved with uncertainty and quality flags. NASA Images records carry per-image credits in public/nasa/catalog.json. JPL Horizons result includes its ephemeris header and source identifiers.

JavaScript dependencies are listed with exact resolved versions in package-lock.json. License texts for runtime/build dependencies are collected in THIRD_PARTY/dependency_licenses.
Texture authors: aerial_rocks_02 — Rob Tuytel. rock_face_03 — Dario Barresi (photography), Rico Cilliers (processing). Poly Haven metadata snapshots are retained.

## Additional November 13 integration

Boulder 01 photogrammetry: Rico Cilliers / Poly Haven, CC0. https://polyhaven.com/a/boulder_01 . Scanned mesh, PBR textures and a decimated instancing LOD are included; display albedo is recolored for the modeled Martian scene, not a measured Mars sample.

Six character heads use the actual MakeHuman Community base topology and six distinct macro morph targets, all core assets under CC0. https://www.makehumancommunity.org/about/license.html . NASA Z2 suit geometry is individually fitted and skinned for Leon, Ethan, Orion, Sadia, Amelia and Noor. Animation clips are shared by the six rigs. They are original fictional derivatives, not likenesses of real named astronauts.
